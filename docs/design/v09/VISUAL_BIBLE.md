# v0.9 Visual Bible

Goal: make Maccabist feel like a football career videogame, not a dashboard.

Layering: cinematic background -> age/position-correct player -> effects -> glass/metal coded UI -> dynamic DOM data.

Home: large player hero, next match hero, career feed.
Matchday: cinematic score stage, progressive timeline, player moments, full-time reveal.
Decisions: full-screen career choices with club/contract/role/Europe/agent opinion.
Career: life-story timeline and prestigious trophy presentation.

Do not bake dynamic content into images and do not rewrite the simulation engine to fit the redesign.
